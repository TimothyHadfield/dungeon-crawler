How to Play

Descend into the Abyssal Depths. Reach Floor 10 and slay the Abyssal Dragon to win.

Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys — Move / Attack
  • Space — Wait one turn (regen 1 HP)
  • E — Use stairs (when standing on ◇)
  • 1-8 — Use item / Shift+1-8 — Drop item
  • Tab — Auto-explore (move toward nearest unexplored area)
  • ? or H — Toggle help
  • M — Toggle sound

Gameplay

  • Bump to attack — walk into enemies to fight
  • Walk over items to pick them up
  • Find the glowing stairs ◇ on each floor to descend
  • Enemies grow stronger the deeper you go
  • Leveling up lets you choose a stat boost
  • Weapons and armor auto-equip if better than current
  • Watch out for hidden spike traps on the floor!
  • Water pools slow enemies but not you

Enemies

  • 🐀 Rats — Weak, appear early
  • 🐍 Snakes — Quick, venomous
  • 💀 Skeletons — Balanced fighters
  • 👹 Orcs — Strong brutes
  • 👻 Wraiths — Ethereal, hard to hit
  • 🗿 Golems — Massive HP, heavy armor
  • 😈 Demons — Deadly attackers
  • 🐉 Abyssal Dragon — Final boss on Floor 10

Items

  • ❤️ Health Potions — Restore HP
  • ⚔️ Weapons — Increase ATK (auto-equip if better)
  • 🛡️ Armor — Increase DEF (auto-equip if better)
  • ⚡ Power / Shield Scrolls — Temporary stat boosts
  • 💎 Gems — Bonus gold
